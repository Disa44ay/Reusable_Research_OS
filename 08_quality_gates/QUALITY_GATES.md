# Quality Gates

## Before topic lock
Dataset access is verified. Exact benchmark is known. A credible baseline exists. Critical modalities are confirmed. Compute is feasible. The research gap has evidence. The evaluation protocol is objective.

## Before coding the contribution
The baseline can be reproduced or a documented fallback exists. The proposed intervention is defined. Metrics and ablations are frozen enough to prevent result-driven goal shifting.

## Before claiming improvement
Results come from controlled experiments. Baselines are fair. Data leakage has been checked. Failures and negative results are preserved. Runtime and compute are recorded where relevant.

## Before manuscript submission
Every citation exists. Dataset and license claims are verified. Claimed novelty is appropriately scoped. Figures and tables match actual results. Reproducibility instructions exist. Unsupported statements are removed or labeled.

## Graph integrity gate
Before a checkpoint is considered durable:
1. every major current decision links to the constraints or evidence that caused it,
2. rejected and superseded ideas remain discoverable,
3. new candidate topics link to datasets and evidence nodes,
4. no internal Obsidian link is unresolved,
5. the readable narrative and atomic nodes do not contradict each other without an explicit supersession link.

## External AI quality gate
A finding from Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, Copilot, or another assistant cannot become VERIFIED solely because multiple assistants repeat it. At least one load-bearing primary source or official repository must support it.

A novelty claim should state the exact searched boundary and avoid converting "not found" into "does not exist."
