# Topic Scoring and Rejection

## Score dimensions
Publication potential, novelty, dataset availability, multimodality, compute feasibility, implementation difficulty, implementation time, baseline availability, reproducibility, industry relevance, backend opportunity, AI or CV learning value, portfolio value, and overall risk.

## Reject a topic when
1. The dataset is inaccessible.
2. The task requires unavailable compute.
3. No credible baseline exists.
4. Novelty is only a trivial hyperparameter change.
5. The claimed gap cannot be supported by literature.
6. The experiment cannot finish inside the actual execution window.
7. The area is saturated and no meaningful new angle is supported.

## Contribution types that may be defensible
Multimodal fusion, lightweight or efficient modeling, temporal modeling, context integration, robustness improvement, tracking or action association, deployment optimization, latency or accuracy trade-off, and reproducible end-to-end systems.

A contribution does not require inventing a new neural architecture.

## Additional operational dimensions
Also score:
1. data-access latency,
2. storage burden,
3. research-compute budget,
4. deployment recurring cost,
5. annotation burden,
6. team-capacity fit,
7. supervisor or disciplinary fit,
8. system-integration opportunity,
9. whether multimodality is scientifically necessary or decorative,
10. deadline risk for obtaining a defensible initial title.

## Additional rejection conditions
Reject or heavily downgrade a topic when:
1. the final demonstration requires recurring paid infrastructure and zero-cost deployment is a hard project constraint,
2. success depends on manual annotation beyond the team's realistic annotation ceiling,
3. dataset approval delay can block the project and no fallback exists,
4. the supposed multimodal contribution adds no measurable information,
5. the system engineering layer is being used to disguise a weak research contribution.
