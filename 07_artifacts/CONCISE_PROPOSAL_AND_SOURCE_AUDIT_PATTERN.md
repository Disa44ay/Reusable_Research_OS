# Concise Proposal and Source Audit Pattern

A complete laboratory record and a readable research proposal serve different purposes.

## Proposal

The proposal should present only the material needed to judge:

1. problem and motivation,
2. verified gap,
3. research questions,
4. data,
5. method and experiment design,
6. feasibility,
7. expected contribution and limitations.

## Source audit

Keep a separate source/claim audit recording:

1. primary source,
2. exact claim supported,
3. qualification or scope boundary,
4. corrections to earlier wording,
5. time-sensitive platform/dataset notes.

This preserves evidence depth without turning the proposal into the entire research notebook.
