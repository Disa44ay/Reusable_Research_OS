# Candidate Title Checkpoint

Use this checkpoint when a supervisor or institution requires an initial title before the research topic is fully validated.

## Required inputs
1. A bounded domain.
2. At least one accessible or realistically accessible dataset.
3. At least one primary paper or benchmark establishing the task.
4. A candidate weakness or research question.
5. A feasible compute path.
6. A measurable evaluation path.

## Output
Prefer 2 to 3 candidate titles when allowed.

Each candidate must include:
1. working title,
2. research question,
3. expected contribution type,
4. dataset path,
5. baseline path,
6. novelty status,
7. compute risk,
8. data-access risk,
9. annotation burden,
10. deployment path when deployment is a project objective,
11. exact reasons it could be rejected later.

## Rule
A candidate title is not a topic lock. The title is a controlled hypothesis that may be narrowed or replaced after deeper literature validation.
